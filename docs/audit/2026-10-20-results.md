## Independent review and corrective regressions

A separate read-only reviewer inspected the whole branch at `c9f7b7e`. Four Important findings were addressed: slow HTTP responses bypassing socket timeout, abandoned runners losing elapsed-budget accounting, retesting invalidating an unchanged installation snapshot, and full-storage probes disabling read/export/delete recovery. Duplicate ZIP members were additionally treated as Important because dictionary conversion could discard source. Each received a regression that fails before the repair and passes afterward; the complete suite and WebKit flow pass after the fixes.

The model transport now uses a trusted bounded helper process for an absolute deadline. A mission reserves remaining elapsed allowance before running; a crashed/abandoned lease conservatively consumes it on reclamation. This may block a mission after a brief crash, but cannot reset its allowance. Snapshots validate their original successful local receipt independently from the latest successful receipt. No deferred review findings remain.
